How it works
01Your account

Funds stay in your Hyperliquid account.

The bot connects with an API wallet. It can place orders. It cannot withdraw.

02Long spot

The bot buys spot ETH.

75% of your capital goes into spot ETH. Exposure: Δ +1.

03Short perp

Then it shorts the same amount of ETH-PERP.

The other 25% is margin for the short, at most 3×. Exposure: Δ −1. Net: 0.

04Delta 0

ETH moves. Your position doesn't.

The spot leg gains what the short loses, and the other way round.

05Collect the carry

Get paid to hold the hedge.

Perpetual futures pay a funding rate. When the market leans long, longs pay shorts. Your short collects that payment every hour, straight into your account. Funding varies and can turn negative.

Funding → your accountPaid hourly
06Automated

The bot keeps it at zero.

It watches price, delta and margin around the clock. When ETH moves and the hedge drifts more than 2%, it adjusts the short with limit orders until delta is back at zero.

When a rally eats into the short's margin, it sells part of the spot gain and moves it over. No manual steps.

07Specifications

Bot 01, in numbers.

  1. 01

    Capital efficiency 0.75×

    At the 3× short cap, 75% of capital sits in spot and earns funding: 1 ÷ (1 + 1/3). The other 25% is margin for the short.

  2. 02

    Post-only limit orders

    Entries and rebalances go out as add-liquidity-only orders, so you pay maker fees: 0.015% on perps and 0.040% on spot instead of 0.045% and 0.070% (Hyperliquid base tier).

  3. 03

    Event-driven rebalancing

    No fixed schedule. The bot trades only when delta drifts outside a ±2% band or margin drops below its floor, so it doesn't pay fees to stand still.

  4. 04

    Liquidation distance ~31%

    At 3×, ETH has to rise about 31% before the short is liquidated. The bot moves spot gains into margin long before that.
