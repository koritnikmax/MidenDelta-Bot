Questions

Before you subscribe.

Does MidenDelta ever hold my money?

No. The bot runs on your machine and trades in your own Hyperliquid account through an API wallet that can't withdraw. We never receive, hold or move your funds, and we never see your keys.

What happens when I cancel?

The licence runs until the end of the paid period. After that the bot stops opening new positions and offers to close your existing ones in one step. It never leaves you half-hedged. Your funds stay in your account the whole time.

What returns can I expect?

We don't promise any. The bot earns the funding rate on its short, which changes every hour and can turn negative. Our simulated research shows how the strategy would have behaved historically; the future will differ.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can install an app and copy a key, you can run the bot. The video course and the AI helper take you through every step, including creating the API wallet.

Where can I use it?

Wherever you can legally use Hyperliquid. Hyperliquid restricts some regions, including the United States; its terms apply to your account.

Why no performance fee?

Because we sell software, not asset management. A flat price keeps our incentives simple: we're paid for a bot that works well and is easy to run, not for how much you trade or risk.

Can I get a refund?

For consumers in the EU, the statutory right of withdrawal applies. The details will be in our terms at launch.
