AI helper

Stuck? Ask.

The helper knows the course, the documentation and, if you share it, your bot's log. It explains what the bot just did, walks you through setup and helps you fix errors.

You'll be talking to an AI, not a person. It explains how the software works; it doesn't give investment advice or tell you how much to invest. Coming with early access.

Example
The bot says "margin top-up 1,200 USDC". Did I lose money?
No. ETH rose, so your short lost value while your spot ETH gained the same amount. The bot sold 1,200 USDC of the spot gain and moved it to the short's margin, so it stays far from liquidation. Your total is unchanged, apart from funding and fees.
Should I raise leverage to earn more?
I can't advise you on that. What I can explain: higher leverage puts more of your capital into the trade, but leaves the short with less margin, so a sharp ETH rally gets it closer to liquidation. Lesson 06 covers the trade-off.
